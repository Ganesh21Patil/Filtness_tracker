# Business Goals & Target Audience
### Personal Trainer / Fitness Coach Tax Calculator

Keep this alongside the other two prompts. Paste it into Claude Code/ChatGPT whenever you're asking for homepage copy, marketing angles, or feature-priority decisions — it gives the AI the "why" behind the build, not just the "what."

```
BUSINESS GOAL
This site is a free tax calculator for US personal trainers and fitness
coaches, monetized through display ads (starting with AdSense, moving to
Ezoic/Mediavine once traffic qualifies) and affiliate partnerships with
services this exact audience already needs. It should be built as a real,
durable asset — clean and documented enough that it could be sold later —
not a thin content site chasing quick ad clicks. Start with one flagship
calculator; expand to adjacent tools for the same audience once it has
traffic.

TARGET AUDIENCE
Self-employed / 1099 personal trainers and fitness coaches in the US, in
three overlapping groups:
- Gym-affiliated contractors who pay a booth/rental fee or revenue split
  to a studio
- Fully independent trainers running their own studio or online coaching
  business
- Hybrid trainers with a W-2 gym paycheck plus private 1099 clients

What's true about this audience and matters for how the site should feel:
- Physically active, time-poor, usually checking things on a phone
  between client sessions — not sitting at a desk with a spreadsheet
- Generally not financially sophisticated and often anxious about
  "am I even saving enough for taxes" — many have never had a
  self-employed tax conversation before
- Skeptical of anything that feels like a hard sell or a corporate tax
  firm — they respond better to a peer/community tone than a formal one
- Where they actually gather online: fitness-professional subreddits,
  NASM/ACE/ISSA certification alumni groups, fitness-business podcasts
  and communities (e.g. PTDC-style resources), gym-owner Facebook groups,
  fitness-business creators on Instagram/TikTok

HOW TO MAKE THE SITE APPEALING TO THIS AUDIENCE (apply to copy, design,
and feature decisions)
- Tone: encouraging and reassuring, never judgmental about not knowing
  tax basics already — e.g. "Most trainers miss these deductions, let's
  find yours" rather than dense financial jargon
- Zero friction: full result visible with no signup wall; only ask for
  an email as an opt-in for a bonus PDF, never as a gate to the core
  calculator
- Visual credibility without corporate sterility: cite real IRS sources
  and show a last-updated date, but keep the visual language energetic
  and fitness-industry-appropriate, not beige and stiff
- Launch into the actual communities listed above with a genuine
  "I built this free tool, feedback welcome" post before leaning on SEO
  — this audience trusts a peer recommendation far more than a cold
  Google result, and it's the fastest way to real first users
- Affiliate placements should be things this audience already needs and
  will recognize: liability insurance providers, coaching/scheduling
  software (Trainerize, TrueCoach), certification renewal or CEU
  providers — surfaced naturally inside the deduction walkthrough, not
  as generic banner ads
- Natural v2+ expansion ideas that stay inside this same audience rather
  than diluting focus: a simple income/invoice tracker, a certification
  renewal reminder tool, an LLC vs. S-corp comparison for higher earners,
  a mileage log tool — each deepens the site's value to the same visitor
  instead of chasing a new one

IF THIS SITE IS LATER SOLD AS AN ASSET
Buyers of small niche sites want to see a clean, consistent record from
day one — so from month one, keep a simple log of monthly traffic and
revenue (analytics exports are enough), and avoid revenue that depends on
a single ad network alone. Diversified, documented, and steadily growing
beats a short traffic spike every time.
```
